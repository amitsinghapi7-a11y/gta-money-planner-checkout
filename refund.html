Refund Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests are handled for GTA Money Planner Pro purchases.

1. Payment Processing

Payments for GTA Money Planner Pro are processed by Paddle, which acts as the payment provider and Merchant of Record where applicable.

Refunds are subject to applicable consumer protection laws and Paddle's applicable payment and refund procedures.

2. Pro Monthly Subscriptions

Pro Monthly is a recurring subscription billed at the price and frequency shown during checkout.

You may cancel your subscription to prevent future renewals. Cancellation does not automatically mean that a refund will be issued for a previous payment.

Refund requests for subscription charges may be submitted for review, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the request.

3. Pro Lifetime Purchases

Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew automatically.

If you experience a billing problem, an accidental duplicate charge or a significant technical issue that prevents access to the purchased features, you may submit a refund request for review.

4. Duplicate or Unauthorized Payments

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or you notice a payment you did not authorize, contact Paddle or our support team as soon as possible.

We will review the available transaction information and assist with the appropriate resolution.

5. Technical Problems

If a purchased Pro feature is not working as described, contact our support team before submitting a refund request.

We will make reasonable efforts to investigate and resolve verified technical problems.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, use the payment support or customer support options provided by Paddle for your transaction, or contact GTA Money Planner support at the address listed below.

Support email: gtamoneyplanner.support@gmail.com

Please include your transaction ID, purchase email address and a short explanation of the issue. Never send full payment card details.

7. Refund Decisions and Processing

Refund requests are reviewed individually. Approved refunds are processed through the applicable payment provider.

Processing times may depend on Paddle, the payment method and the financial institution.

8. Statutory Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact

For refund-related questions, contact us using the support details listed above or the support options provided by Paddle.
