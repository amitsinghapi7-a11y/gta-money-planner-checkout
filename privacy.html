Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA Money Planner collects, uses and handles information when you use our product.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GTA Money Planner, we may process the following information:

  • Account information: Email address and authentication information used to create and manage your account.
  • Game planning data: Virtual money balances, goals, planned purchases, transaction history and money-making methods that you choose to save.
  • Membership information: Subscription plan, payment status and transaction identifiers required to manage Pro access.
  • Technical information: Information necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Save and synchronize game-planning data across supported sessions.
  • Manage Free and Pro membership access.
  • Process subscription and payment-related events.
  • Maintain security and troubleshoot technical problems.

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

GTA Money Planner uses Supabase for account authentication and cloud storage of user game-planning data.

Our backend uses Supabase services to verify accounts and manage membership information.

We use Paddle to process payments and manage billing. Payment card information is handled by Paddle through its payment services and is not intentionally stored in our GTA Money Planner database.

These providers process information according to their applicable privacy policies and service terms.

4. Payment Information

When purchasing a Pro membership, payment information is collected and processed through Paddle's checkout system.

We may receive payment-related identifiers, customer information and subscription status needed to activate, maintain or cancel Pro access.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Supabase and Paddle when necessary to provide authentication, cloud storage, payment processing and membership services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

6. Data Security

We use security measures including authenticated account access, database access controls and protected backend credentials to help safeguard information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Information is retained as necessary to provide the service, maintain account records, handle payments and meet applicable legal obligations.

If you want to request account or personal data deletion, contact us using the support contact provided on our official checkout page. Some records may need to be retained where required by law.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or request information about your personal data.

You may contact us to make a privacy-related request.

9. Children's Privacy

GTA Money Planner is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

10. Third-Party Services

Our product uses third-party services, including Supabase and Paddle. Their own privacy policies govern their processing of information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Changes will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact us at gtamoneyplanner.support@gmail.com.
